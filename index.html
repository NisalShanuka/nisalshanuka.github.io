Anonymous Copy-Trading Platform

Solo founder and developer · Live since Oct 2026

Reads trade signals from a Telegram VIP channel and places the same trade on every subscriber's own exchange account within seconds.

Reads the signal

A Telegram listener understands short, informal admin posts, even Singlish split across messages. An AI verifier (Gemini, with Groq as backup) checks every entry before anything trades.

Copies it to every account

Places the trade on each customer's own Binance, Bybit or MEXC futures account, sized to their balance, margin, leverage and take-profit choice.

Manages it to the close

Stop-loss, take-profit ladders, DCA, break-even after TP1 and partial closes follow the admin in real time, with alerts in Sinhala and English.
